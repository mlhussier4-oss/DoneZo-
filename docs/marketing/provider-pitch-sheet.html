Why pros switch to HomeHire

  • No pay-per-lead. One flat price. Bid on as many jobs as you like.
  • Real jobs, not mystery leads. Every job comes with photos, the homeowner’s answers (size, rooms, stairs and so on) and a ZIP code, so you can price it before you bid.
  • Sealed bids. Other pros never see your price, so nobody undercuts you in public.
  • Serious customers. Homeowners pick one bid and you confirm the job in minutes. No chasing people who requested ten quotes.
  • Get paid your way. Cash, Venmo, Cash App and check carry no HomeHire fee. Card payments through HomeHire: 10%.
  • Instant job alerts on your phone the moment a job is posted near you.
  • Stand out. Add photos of your work, collect reviews from real jobs, and show an “Insured” badge if you carry insurance.

How it works

  1. Set up your profile in a few minutes: services, service area, photos of your work.
  2. Get alerts when a homeowner nearby posts a job with photos.
  3. Send a sealed bid with your price and when you can do it.
  4. Get picked, confirm within 5 minutes and set your arrival time. A private chat with the customer opens automatically, where they send you their address and you sort out the details.
  5. Finish and get paid with one tap: card through HomeHire, or cash, Venmo, Cash App or check.
