How it works

  1. Post your jobChoose a service, add photos and answer a few quick questions.
  2. Compare bidsLocal pros send sealed bids. They appear on your dashboard as they arrive.
  3. Pick your proThey confirm your job and arrival time within minutes. A private chat opens so you can send your address.
  4. Pay after the jobBy card through HomeHire, or cash, Venmo, Cash App or check. Then rate your pro.
